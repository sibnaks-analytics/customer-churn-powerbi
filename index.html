889 customers are likely to leave. $61.9K a month leaves with them.

I built a three-page Power BI dashboard on 7,043 telecom customers to find out why people leave, which groups are most at risk, and how much monthly revenue is exposed.

What I would do about it

  • Offer incentives to move month-to-month customers onto one- or two-year contracts. They churn at 42.7%, against 2.8% on two-year contracts.
  • Focus onboarding on the first 12 months, when churn reaches 47.4%.
  • Promote tech support to customers who don’t have it.
  • Encourage a move from electronic check to automatic payment.
  • Contact the highest-paying high-risk customers first.
